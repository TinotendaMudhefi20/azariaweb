WHO WE ARE

A place to learn,
grow and belong.

Azariah Christian College is a Christian secondary school in Borrowdale, Harare, offering Forms 1–6, day scholar and boarding facilities, and Cambridge and ZIMSEC curriculum pathways.

We believe education should prepare young people not only for examinations, but also for life. Our country-school setting creates opportunities to learn, explore and discover.
