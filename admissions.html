Forms: 1–6

Curricula: Cambridge & ZIMSEC

Options: Day scholar & boarding

Please contact the school to confirm current availability and entry arrangements.

HOW TO APPLY

Application requirements.

Prepare the following documents when making an application.

01 Copy of the student's birth certificate
02 One recent passport-sized photograph
03 Most recent school reports
04 Relevant academic, sporting or cultural certificates, where applicable

Application process

  1. Submit the completed application form and supporting documents.
  2. Successful applicants receive an admission offer.
  3. Pay the acceptance deposit to secure the place.
  4. Complete enrolment and receive the school handbook, fee schedule, uniform requirements and stationery list.
FEES & ENQUIRIES

Plan your child's education.

Contact the admissions office for the latest approved fee schedule and additional charges.

Day scholars

Ask the school about current tuition and day scholar arrangements.

Boarding

Ask about boarding fees, availability and residential arrangements.

Additional charges

Request details of registration, examinations, insurance and other applicable charges.

Fees and charges may be reviewed. Please obtain the current official fee schedule directly from the school before making payments.
