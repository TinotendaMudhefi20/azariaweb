About Azariah.

Empowering Posterity for a Better Tomorrow.

OUR STORY

A country-school experience
in the heart of Harare.

Azariah Christian College is a Christian secondary school located at Plot 1 Killarney Estate, Borrowdale, Harare.

We offer day scholar and boarding facilities for Forms 1–6, with Cambridge and ZIMSEC curriculum pathways. Our learning environment seeks to develop confident problem-solvers, compassionate individuals and responsible citizens.

OUR VISION

Confident, compassionate citizens.

To provide a safe, supportive and Christ-centred learning community that develops confident problem-solvers, compassionate individuals and responsible citizens.

OUR MISSION

Empowering posterity.

Empowering posterity for a better tomorrow through education, character development and purposeful learning.
