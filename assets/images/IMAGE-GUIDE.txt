AZARIAH CHRISTIAN COLLEGE - IMAGE GUIDE

All website photographs are supplied as high-quality WebP copies.
The uploaded originals have not been altered.

Folders:
- logo/       Official school crest used in the navigation bar.
- students/   Student portraits and uniform photographs.
- academics/  Science practical lessons and classroom learning.
- activities/ Outdoor challenges, team building and sports.
- campus/     School grounds and campus life.
- events/     School gatherings and admissions activities.
- gallery/    Additional group photographs.

To replace a photo:
1. Put the new optimised photo in the relevant folder.
2. Open the HTML page and search for the image filename.
3. Change only the filename/path and the alt description.
4. Keep the folder structure unchanged.

The image files are not cropped or resized. WebP encoding is high quality;
the uploaded originals remain separate and unchanged.
